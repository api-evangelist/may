# May

> May is a French digital-health company, operated by LN CARE SAS, whose mobile app supports
> future and new parents from fertility and pregnancy through the first years of a child's
> life. It pairs a medical messaging service staffed by French-qualified midwives, pediatric
> nurses and doctors — available seven days a week from 8am to 10pm — with an expert-reviewed
> content library, audio masterclasses, everyday tracking tools for sleep, feeding and weight,
> and Mya, an in-app AI medical assistant. Founded in Paris in 2020 by Cécilia Creuzet,
> Antoine Creuzet and Adrien Brunet, it raised €7M in September 2024 to expand into Spain and
> the United Kingdom, and also sells a parental-support benefit to employers.

## API surface

There is none. As of 2026-08-17 the following were probed on every May host and all returned
HTTP 404: `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`,
`/docs`, `/redoc`, `/api`, `/graphql`, `/mcp`, `/llms.txt`, and the whole `/.well-known/`
discovery surface (`security.txt`, `openid-configuration`, `oauth-authorization-server`,
`oauth-protected-resource`, `api-catalog`, `ai-plugin.json`, `agent-card.json`, `agent.json`).
The subdomains `api.`, `app.`, `developer.` and `docs.may.app` do not resolve. The only
backend hostname anywhere in the company's public DNS is `capig.may.app`, found in
certificate-transparency logs, which is a dangling CNAME to a deleted AWS Kubernetes ingress
load balancer in eu-west-3 and cannot be reached at all. No first-party SDK exists on npm,
PyPI or any other registry, and there is no GitHub organization for the company.

The only machine-readable HTTP surface on the domain is the default WordPress core REST API at
`https://www.may.app/wp-json/` — CMS infrastructure WordPress ships with every installation.
It is not designed, documented or supported by May as a developer API and is not catalogued
as one.
